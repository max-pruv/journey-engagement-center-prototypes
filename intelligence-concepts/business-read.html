Shoppers

Four groups carry this business. They are not equal.

Grouped by what they do, not who they are. The engine treats each group differently because each one buys for a different reason.

System builders

Start with a shell, add layers within two months. Highest LTV, most predictable, cheapest to keep.

Best move: complete their system before day 54 — never discount them.

41%of customers

Winter regulars

Return every cold season for one technical piece. Buy full price when the forecast drops.

Best move: reach them on the first sub-zero forecast with technical detail.

23%of customers

Gift buyers

One order a year, November–December. Never return, and that is fine — they are profitable if acquisition stays cheap.

Best move: gift guides and delivery dates in Q4; leave them alone the rest of the year.

21%of customers

Discount hunters

Only buy on promotion. Every euro spent retaining them costs more than they return.

Best move: exclude from loyalty spend; they get the public sale, nothing earlier.

15%of customers

01 · scale

Compatibility beats novelty.

Every winning move starts from what the shopper already owns. Make that the default reason for every second-order message.

02 · stop

Price is the last lever, not the first.

The best customers never need a discount; the worst customers only need a discount. Treat them differently or pay for both.

03 · time

The calendar is the strategy.

Pre-cold cross-sell now, gifting in six weeks, silence in spring. The engine schedules around these, not around campaign dates.
