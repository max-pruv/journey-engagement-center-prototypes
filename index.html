Themes and the plays underneath them

6 of 8 allowed 2 off · ≈$19,400 GMV left on the table

Two layers. A theme is what ACE is allowed to work on — the control that matters if you need to bound an autonomous system. Under each one sit the plays it runs: the standard engagements, now owned by the engine. Each play can be switched off on its own without pulling the whole theme, and still carries exactly one skill. Turn a theme off and every play under it stops regardless of its own switch: it won't propose them, won't send them, won't count them. We keep sizing the opportunity anyway, so you can see what you're declining. Click a row to expand it.
